answer people who comment back. The link is:

**One rule for all of it: never claim XR is out yet.** It is not
released. Every line below says so, because the people in your comments
will check, and because a waitlist only works if people believe you will
actually email them. The site's own copy follows the same rule — that is
why it says "XR isn't released yet — that is what the waitlist is for"
instead of an install command.

## 5. Things not to say

| Don't | Why | Say instead |
|---|---|---|
| "Download it now" / "npm i -g @rrrtx/xr" | It is not released. The published npm artifact is older than the build the site shows, so telling people to install it hands them something different from what the site shows | "Not out yet — waitlist" |
| "Thousands of you are waiting" | There is no such number. Do not invent one | "The waitlist is open" |
| "Join 500+ developers" | Same — no number is verified | "Join the waitlist" |
| "Free forever, no catch" | The pricing is on the site; let it speak | "Free for personal use — the site has the details" |
| "Your data never leaves your machine" | Only true if someone brings their own key/model | "Local-first, BYOK, no telemetry" |
| "We never collect anything" | The waitlist collects an email address | "One email, and it stays on the list until you delete it" |
| "Reply 'XR' to join" | Adds friction and a fake engagement loop | Give the link |
